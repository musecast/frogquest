Frog Quest+ Support

Thanks for playing Frog Quest+. This page covers common support routes for the iOS app.

Gameplay or App Issues

If something is not working correctly, include the app version, your iOS version, your device model, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful when the issue is visual.

Purchases

Frog Quest+ may offer an optional Remove Ads purchase through Apple. If a purchase does not apply, open the Remove Ads screen in the app and use Restore Purchases. Apple handles billing, receipts, taxes, and refunds.

Privacy

Read the Frog Quest+ Privacy Policy for details about privacy, ads, purchases, and related data practices.

Contact

Use the developer contact options shown on the Frog Quest+ App Store product page. For privacy-specific questions, cite "Frog Quest+ privacy enquiry" and include your locale.
